In short

  • Your voice, screen and camera go straight from your device to Google using your own API key. We do not store them.
  • Your Gemini API key stays on your device. We never receive it.
  • We keep only your account, the memories you ask us to save and what you send to support.
  • Apps you connect (Gmail, Google Calendar, GitHub, Notion, Slack) are used only when you ask. Your sign-in for them stays encrypted on your phone, and nothing is sent or created without your yes.
  • We never sell your data, never use it to train AI, and never make payments for you.
  • You can see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to us.

Voice, screen and camera

When a conversation is active, your voice is streamed in real time from your device directly to Google's Gemini service using your own API key. Your screen or camera is captured only when your request is about them, for example "what's on my screen?". These recordings and images are not stored on Aethon Chat servers. Google processes them under its own terms and privacy policy, which apply to your API key.

Photos, files and voice typing in Chat (Android)

In Chat you can take a photo with your camera, pick a photo from your gallery, attach a file (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel or text) or type by voice. A photo or file is copied into the app's private storage on your phone and is sent to Google's Gemini service, with your own API key, only when you send it in a message. Photos are made smaller on your phone first. When you take a photo in Aethon Chat, the camera's own copy is removed again, so it does not stay in your gallery. These photos and files are not stored on Aethon Chat servers, and they are deleted from the app when you delete the chat. Voice typing uses your phone's speech service (usually Google's), which turns your speech into text before it reaches Aethon Chat.

Saved chats and the Research agent

Your chats are saved only on your phone, so you can open them again from the chat list; deleting a chat removes it. When you ask the Research agent, Gemini may search the web with Google Search to answer, under Google's terms. The search is made by Google, not by Aethon Chat.

Location

We use your location only when you ask for something nearby, such as movie shows, restaurants or hospitals, and only for that request.

Contacts and calls (Android)

When you ask Aethon Chat to call or message someone, the app looks up that contact on your phone so it can place the call. We do not upload your contact list to our servers.

Audit Files

Client files you add to the Audit Files workspace are stored on your own PC. If you turn on text reading for scanned pages, those pages are sent to Google Gemini one page at a time for that purpose only. Client data is never used to train AI models.

Website

The website does not use advertising or tracking cookies and does not run analytics. It stores one small item in your browser to remember the latest app version for an hour. When the page loads, fonts are fetched from Google Fonts and the latest version number from GitHub, so those providers can see your IP address.

Updates

The desktop app checks GitHub for new versions. GitHub can see your IP address when it does.

03What we never do

  • We never sell your personal data or share it for advertising.
  • We never use your conversations, memories, screen, camera or files to train AI models.
  • We never complete payments on your behalf. Aethon Chat stops at the payment page and you pay yourself.
  • We never look at your screen or camera unless your request is about them.
  • We never receive your Gemini API key.

04Legal basis

We process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you create an account, start a conversation or send us a message, and for the legitimate uses allowed by the DPDP Act, such as complying with law or responding to a request you started.

You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the data in the app, deleting your account or writing to us. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before, and some features will stop working without the data they need.

05Who we share data with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run the Services, and only as much as they need:

  • Google (Gemini): processes your voice, text, screen and camera requests under your own API key and Google's terms.
  • Our authentication and database provider (Supabase): hosts accounts, memories and phone link data.
  • GitHub: hosts the app installer and update files.
  • Our payment provider: processes subscription payments.
  • Our email provider (Google Gmail): receives support and business messages.
  • Our website host (Vercel): runs the support form and the connector sign-in step described below. It does not store your content.

We may also disclose data when Indian law requires it, to respond to a valid legal order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public. If Aethon Chat is ever merged or sold, your data would move only under a policy at least as protective as this one, and we would tell you first.

Apps and websites that Aethon Chat opens for you, such as Spotify, YouTube, WhatsApp, movie and food platforms, are run by other companies under their own privacy policies.

Connected apps (Gmail, Google Calendar, GitHub, Notion, Slack)

In the Android app you can connect other apps in Settings → Connectors. Connecting is always your choice and you can disconnect at any time.

  • How you connect: you sign in on the app's own page (for example Google's). Aethon Chat never sees your password. Our website only swaps the one-time sign-in code for an access token (this step needs a secret that cannot be stored in the phone app), encrypts the token with a key that only your phone has, and passes it straight back to your phone. Our servers do not keep the token or any of your content.
  • Where it is kept: the access token is stored in your phone's encrypted storage. Uninstalling the app or tapping Disconnect removes it. You can also remove Aethon Chat's access in your Google account (Security → Your connections to third-party apps), GitHub, Notion or Slack settings.
  • What Aethon Chat reads, and when: only what you ask for, at the moment you ask – for example your unread emails, today's calendar events, your open pull requests, a Notion page or a Slack channel. To answer you, that content is sent to Google Gemini under your own API key, in the same way as any other request.
  • Actions: sending an email, creating a calendar event, opening an issue, creating a page or posting a message is always shown to you first and happens only after you say yes.
  • What we never do with it: we do not sell it, use it for advertising, use it to train AI models, or let people read it. We do not move it to any other service except as described here.
  • Google user data: Aethon Chat's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

06Data outside India

Some of our providers store or process data on servers outside India. We transfer data only to countries that are not restricted by the Government of India under the DPDP Act, and we choose providers that protect data with industry-standard security.

07How long we keep data

  • Account data and memories: for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete it within 30 days, except what we must keep by law.
  • Phone link commands: only as long as needed to deliver them.
  • Support and business emails: up to 24 months after the conversation ends, so we can refer back to it.
  • Payment records: as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • Data on your own device (tasks, settings, API key, Audit Files): until you delete it or uninstall the app.

08How we protect data

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit, signed sign-in tokens for every account request, and access controls on our database. The engine that controls your PC accepts requests only from your own computer (127.0.0.1), so other devices on your network cannot reach it.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.

09Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Correct, complete or update data that is wrong or out of date.
  • Erase your data when it is no longer needed or you withdraw consent.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if you are not satisfied, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To delete your whole account, follow the steps on Delete your account.

You can delete memories by saying "forget that", or from the app. For anything else, email us from the address on your account with the subject line "Privacy request". We will respond within the time limits set by law.

10Children

The Services are meant for people aged 18 and over. Anyone under 18 may use them only with the verifiable consent of a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly track children or show them targeted advertising. If we learn we hold a child's data without that consent, we will delete it.

11Health information

The health assistant gives general information only. Please do not share more health details than you need for your question. Requests are processed by Google Gemini like any other request and are not stored on our servers.
