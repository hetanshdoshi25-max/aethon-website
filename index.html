How it works

From your voice to a finished task in seconds.

Every request takes the same short path. Nothing runs in the background without you asking for it.

  1. You speak

    Tap the orb and talk normally. You can interrupt it at any time, just like a person.

  2. It understands

    Google's Gemini Live model hears you in real time. If you mention your screen or camera, it looks too.

  3. It acts on your PC

    A small local engine on 127.0.0.1 opens apps, types, controls windows and media. It only listens to your own computer.

  4. It answers out loud

    You hear what it did in a natural voice, and the conversation shows up on screen as text.

    Privacy and control

    Your PC, your key, your decision.

    An assistant that can control your computer has to earn trust. These are the rules Aethon Chat is built on.

    Your own API key

    Your Gemini key is saved in a file on your own PC. It goes only to Google, never to an Aethon Chat server.

    Local-only engine

    The engine that controls your PC accepts requests from 127.0.0.1 only. Other devices on your network cannot reach it.

    You confirm every payment

    Aethon Chat fills carts and picks seats, then stops. It never enters card details or pays on its own.

    Screen only when asked

    Your screen and camera are looked at only when your request is about them, not all the time.

    Memories you can erase

    Memories are tied to your account so phone and PC share them. Ask Aethon Chat to forget anything and it is gone.

    Audit data stays local

    Client files in Audit Files are stored on your PC and are never used to train AI models.

    Setup guide

    Up and running in five minutes

    1. Download the installer

      Get Aethon-Chat-Setup-1.1.0.exe from the download section below.

    2. Allow Windows to run it

      Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen because Aethon Chat is a new app. Click More info, then Run anyway.

    3. Install

      Pick a folder or keep the default. A desktop shortcut and a Start menu entry are created for you.

    4. Sign in

      Open Aethon Chat and create an account or sign in. Your 3-day free trial starts here, and your account keeps your memories and tasks in sync with the phone app.

    5. Add your Gemini API key

      Create a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey, copy it, and paste it into Aethon Chat's API key screen.

    6. Say hello

      Allow microphone access, tap the orb and try "Open YouTube and play lo-fi music". That's it.

      FAQ and troubleshooting

      Questions people ask

      Is Aethon Chat free?

      Every new account gets a 3-day free trial with every feature switched on. After the trial, you continue with a subscription: ₹299 a month, or ₹2,999 a year (instead of ₹3,588, so you save ₹589). Nothing is charged without your confirmation. See Pricing.

      You still use your own Gemini API key for the AI itself. Google offers a free tier with daily limits, and Aethon Chat never resells or marks up that usage.

      I already have a Gemini API key. Why do I need Aethon Chat?

      A Gemini API key is like a SIM card. It gives you access to Google's AI, but on its own it cannot do anything on your computer. Aethon Chat is the phone that the SIM goes into.

      • It acts, not just talks. Gemini alone can only reply with text or speech. Aethon Chat turns your words into more than 35 real actions on your PC: opening apps, typing, controlling windows, volume and music, sending WhatsApp messages and placing calls from your phone.
      • Real-time voice, ready to use. Talk naturally, interrupt it mid-sentence and choose from six voices. With only a key, you would have to build the microphone, speaker and streaming code yourself.
      • It sees when you ask. Say "what's on my screen?" and it looks. No screenshots to take, crop and upload.
      • It remembers you. Memories, tasks and preferences follow you between your PC and your phone. A raw key forgets everything after each request.
      • Ready-made assistants. Movie shows, food orders, news briefings, focus mode, meeting notes, document questions and Audit Files for CA firms are already built in.
      • Safety rules built in. Payments are never automated, the PC engine only listens to your own computer, and your key stays on your PC.

      To get all this from a key alone, you would need to write and maintain thousands of lines of code. Aethon Chat gives it to you in a five-minute install.

      Why do I need a Gemini API key at all?

      Aethon Chat's voice and understanding come from Google's Gemini models. Using your own key means your requests go straight from your PC to Google, under your own Google account and limits. Aethon Chat never resells or marks up that usage.

      Which languages does it understand?

      Aethon Chat understands more than 100 languages, powered by Google Gemini. Speak in English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Spanish, French, Arabic, Japanese and many more, or mix them the way people really talk, like Hinglish. It replies in the language you use.

      Voice replies sound most natural in widely spoken languages; for less common ones, the on-screen text is the most accurate.

      Can I cancel my subscription?

      Yes, at any time. Cancel before your next billing date and you will not be charged again. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

      Does it work on Mac or Linux?

      Not yet. The desktop app is Windows only for now. Aethon Chat for Android is coming soon on Google Play.

      Can I change the voice?

      Yes. There are six voices, male and female, each with its own name and personality. Pick one in the app's settings.

      Is Aethon Chat always listening?

      No. It listens while a conversation is active, after you tap the orb. End the session and the microphone is released.

      Does it watch my screen all the time?

      No. It captures your screen only when your request is about the screen, for example "what's on my screen" or "explain this message".

      Can Aethon Chat spend my money?

      No. For movies and food it prepares everything and then hands the payment page to you. It never enters card details or confirms a payment.

      Where is my API key stored?

      In a settings file on your own PC. It is sent only to Google with each request.

      Why does Windows say it protected my PC?

      Windows shows this for new apps that are not yet widely downloaded. Click More info and then Run anyway. You only see it once.

      How do updates work?

      When a new version is out, Aethon Chat shows an update notice inside the app. Click it to download, and the app restarts on the new version. You never need to download the installer again.

      How do I uninstall it?

      Open Windows Settings, go to Apps, find Aethon Chat and click Uninstall. Your settings stay saved in case you install it again.

      Aethon Chat doesn't hear me

      Check that Windows allows microphone access: Settings, Privacy and security, Microphone, and turn on access for desktop apps. Also check that the right microphone is selected as the default input.

      It says my API key is invalid or the quota is used up

      Copy the key again from Google AI Studio with no extra spaces. If the daily free limit is reached, wait until the next day or create a key in a different Google project.

      It talks but doesn't open apps or control the PC

      The local engine may still be starting. Wait a few seconds after opening Aethon Chat. If it still fails, close Aethon Chat and open it again.

      The conversation stopped after a while

      If the connection drops, tap the orb again to continue. Since version 1.1.0, long conversations reconnect automatically.
